## HOW TO USE THIS LIST

### Method 1: Manual Add (via UI)
1. Go to News → Manage RSS Feeds
2. Click "Add RSS Feed"
3. Paste feed URL
4. Click "Test Feed"
5. Fill in name and category
6. Click "Add Feed"

### Method 2: Bulk Import (via Database)
