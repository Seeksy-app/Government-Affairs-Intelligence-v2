Typography

One family. Clear hierarchy.

Sans 3

Primary and only brand typeface. An open-source humanist sans serif designed for clarity on screens and in dense information environments.

Headlines · 650

Letter spacing −4%. Tight, confident, and sentence case.

Subheads · 700

Use short, useful labels that advance comprehension.

Maintain 1.5–1.7 line height for long-form readability.

Logo system

Compact, direct, and always legible

Clear space

Keep at least half the GA mark width around every side.

Minimum size

Full lockup: 180 px digital. Mark alone: 28 px.

06

Interface language

Actions should feel decisive, not decorative

Book a demoPrimary pathwaySecondary action

Radius

3–6 px. Avoid overly soft, inflated card shapes.

Spacing

Use a disciplined 8 px base rhythm with generous section spacing.

Icons

Simple outlined icons, consistently sized at 16, 20, or 24 px.

Prioritize hierarchy, useful contrast, and fast scanning over visual novelty.

07

Photography and imagery

Show access, institutions, and human connection

Civic architecture should support the message—not overpower it.

Use

Authentic briefings, staff collaboration, government architecture, events, and relationship-building moments.

Treatment

Natural editorial photography with restrained color, documentary lighting, and clear focal points.

Avoid

Abstract AI imagery, glowing orbs, fake dashboards, staged handshakes, flags as decoration, or partisan symbolism.

08

Voice and messaging

Be specific about the path forward

Do

Lead with practical outcomes: find the staffer, identify the event, understand the relationship, reach the target.

Use confident verbs: find, connect, map, monitor, brief, reach.

Don’t

Use vague AI claims, political hype, fear-based urgency, or unprovable superlatives.

Avoid jargon such as revolutionary, game-changing, or intelligence-powered without explanation.

Message hierarchy

Headline: the human outcome.   Support: how aggregated data makes it possible.   CTA: the next concrete step.
